# Code of conduct

Participants should be respectful, precise and constructive. Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, disclosure of another person's private information and deliberate unsafe handling of credentials are unacceptable.

Maintainers may request edits, remove harmful public content or restrict participation, with a proportionate explanation and an opportunity to clarify when safe. Decisions should focus on conduct and protect the privacy of affected people.

Do not publish private allegations or identifying evidence in issues. GitHub Private Vulnerability Reporting is reserved for vulnerabilities; follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). No separate confidential conduct channel or response deadline is established.
